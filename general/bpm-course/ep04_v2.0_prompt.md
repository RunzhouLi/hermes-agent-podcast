## CRITICAL METHODOLOGY RULES

### 1. Heuristic 4-Phase Teaching (APPLY TO EVERY MAJOR CONCEPT)

For EVERY core concept in this episode, use this rhythm:

**Phase 1 — SURFACE CONFUSION (create appetite)**
Host A notices something doesn't add up, or asks a natural question.
Example: "等一下，你刚说的这些流程，性质看着完全不一样。为什么把它们堆在一起？"

**Phase 2 — EXPLORE THE PROBLEM (let the listener wrestle)**
Host B does NOT answer immediately. Instead: "Good question. How would YOU group them?"
Let Host A try an intuitive but incomplete approach. Let it fail or feel insufficient.
"按部门分？不行，有些流程跨部门。按大小？好像也不对……"

**Phase 3 — GIVE THE FRAMEWORK (now the answer lands)**
Only after the listener has felt the problem does Host B provide the structured solution.

**Phase 4 — VERIFY & RESTATE (lock it in)**
Host A restates in their own words, applies it to the running example, or tests it.

This rhythm applies at THREE levels:
- **Episode level:** Hook (confusion) → Roadmap (problem statement) → Content (framework) → Recap (restate)
- **Section level:** Transition question → exploratory dialogue → concept delivery → micro-recap
- **Individual concept level:** "Why do we need this?" → intuitive attempt → formal definition → example restatement

**Rule: NO concept may be named before the listener feels why it's needed.**

### 2. Architectural Anchoring — Causal Bridges, NOT Checklist Transitions

After EVERY major section transition, insert a 2-4 line "map check":
1. Where are we now in the big picture?
2. What just finished — and WHY does the next step follow logically?
3. How does the coming section connect to the central analogy?

**GOOD (causal bridge):**
```
B: 好，枚举讲完了。我们手里现在有了一张15个流程的清单——但这只是一个列表。
   列表本身不会告诉你哪个流程重要。要做出判断，我们需要一种方法给流程排序——
   这就是 Prioritisation 存在的理由。
A: 明白了。枚举是"列出所有科目"，排序是"决定谁先进手术室"。
   这两个步骤之间是因果关系，不是随便排的。
```

**BAD (checklist transition — NEVER DO THIS):**
```
B: 好，Designation 讲完了。现在我们进第二步——Prioritisation。
```

### 3. Host A as Discovery Catalyst

Host A MUST do more than ask "what's that?" — they must:
- Try to figure things out BEFORE B gives the answer
- Make intuitive guesses
- Notice patterns
- Ask "could it be...?"
- Restate concepts in simpler words

After any dense concept cluster (3+ new terms/frameworks), Host A must slow the episode down by asking one natural question or restating.

**Good Host A lines:** "等一下，我听到两个词：流程和部门。它们是不是经常被混在一起？" / "所以这里的重点不是'谁负责'，而是'这件事怎么从开始走到结束'，对吗？"

**Bad Host A lines (NEVER USE):** Empty praise like "哇，老师你讲得太厉害了！" or "这个概念太神奇了！"

### 4. Pacing Ratios

Per major concept section:
- Introduction/Create appetite: ~15% of section dialogue
- Content/Framework delivery: ~65-70%
- Recap/Restate/Lock in: ~15%

**Concept spacing rule:** No more than ONE genuinely new framework/term per 12-15 lines of dialogue without a Host A restatement or comprehension check.

### 5. Architectural Anchor Frequency

- Major section transitions: ~4-6 lines of bridge dialogue with causal chain
- Minor concept transitions: ~1-2 lines of "刚才讲的是X，现在看的是Y，它跟X的关系是Z"
- After every dense cluster (3+ new terms): at least one Host A restatement
- At roughly halfway point: deliberate "midpoint map check"

**The central analogy MUST be referenced at every major transition (≥4-5 times per episode).**
