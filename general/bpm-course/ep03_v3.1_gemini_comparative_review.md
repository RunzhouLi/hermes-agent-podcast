## 5. FACTUAL CORRECTNESS ISSUES

**Issue 1: Contradictory OR Gateway Syntax**
*Snippet from v3.1:*
> B: 跟 XOR 一样，它是"看数据，立刻做决定"。但有一条硬性语法要求：它的每一条出路上，都必须有条件。注意是"所有"出路都要有条件。
> ... (a few lines later) ...
> B: 你说得对，得留活路。... 第二，指定一条"默认流"，英文 default flow。

*Correction:* A default flow explicitly *does not* have a condition. Host B's absolute statement ("所有出路都要有条件") is slightly inaccurate and contradicts the default flow introduction.

**Issue 2: Exercise 3 Semantic Error Misattribution**
*Snippet from v3.1:*
> B: 这题有个埋好的坑——图里出现了"安排餐饮"竟然排在"确认报名人数足够"之前。
> A: 等等，这顺序不对吧？还没确认有没有足够的人报名，就先把餐饮订好了？

*Correction:* Based on the source PDF for Exercise 3, "Room booking" (教室预订) happens before the "Registration website closed" and registration checks. "Catering arranging" actually happens later in the flow. Both v3.0 and v3.1 hallucinate "Catering" as the early mistake. It should be changed to "Room booking" (教室预订).

**Is v3.1 a meaningfully better course replacement than v3.0? Why?**

Yes, absolutely. v3.0's 12-minute runtime was not "legitimately concise"—it was severely under-covered and shallow. It treated complex BPMN concepts (like Soundness and deadlocks) as vocabulary words to be memorized.
